# City parts

City mode's **Parts** group places ready-made pieces of a city — junctions, blocks,
a park, a parking lot, a plaza — onto the open map, and saves selected map content as
the user's own parts (§44.476). Planning is `scripts/city_parts.gd`; the panel is
`scripts/city_parts_panel.gd`; the [workbench](WORKBENCH.md) owns the group and keys.

## Part file v1

A part is one JSON file. The reader is strict: unknown or missing keys, another
`format`/`version`, or malformed geometry are refused; MapKit validates the full
record shapes when the part is placed.

| Key | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `format`, `version` | `"miniearthure-city-part"`, `1` |
| `id`, `name` | `[a-z0-9-]`, ≤64 characters; display name ≤256 |
| `license` | Licence of the part content (`MIT` built-in, `LicenseRef-user` saved) |
| `attributions` | `{source, license, notice}` credits merged into the target map; a notice may not be a JSON object (import metadata) |
| `footprint` | Ring of 3–512 `[x, y]` points |
| `records` | `nodes`, `roads`, `buildings`, `zones`, `surface_areas`, `barriers`, `placements` in MapDocument v1 shapes with local IDs (`[A-Za-z0-9_.-]`, ≤64, unique) |
| `mouths` | `{node, heading_deg, width_cm}`: a road end other roads may join; the node is used by exactly one part road; heading is outward, counter-clockwise from +x |

Coordinates are integer **real-scale source centimetres** around the part's local
origin (x east, y north, `[x, height, y]` vertices); heights are above local ground.
The scalar fields placement scales (widths, heights, bases, clearances, sidewalks,
lane/median widths, spacing, barrier sizes, placement turns) must be integers.

## Placing a part

Choosing a part arms plan placement (the plan opens as a split view when hidden); a
ghost of the footprint, roads and buildings follows the pointer, a click places it and
the tool stays armed. Right-click, Escape, another group or a view change stop it.

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Angle field | Free rotation, 0.1° steps, counter-clockwise |
| Q / E | +15° / −15° (roam context, only while a part is armed) |
| Shift + wheel | ±1° (also the horizontal wheel macOS sends with Shift) |
| Align to road | Turns the part so a mouth (or, without mouths, its axes) follows the road nearest the pointer, choosing the closest such angle |
| Replace objects under the part | On by default |

One placement is one `authoring_tools.apply` command: MapKit document validation,
road/junction cell checks, the hidden/locked layer gate, then one Undo.

1. **Transform.** Rotate about the origin, divide by the map's 1:D (`osm_scale`; 1
   without an OSM layer), translate to the clicked plan point and lift heights onto
   the ground there (terrain height when the map has terrain, otherwise
   `terrain_base_cm`). Widths, building heights, clearances, sidewalks, spacing,
   barrier sizes and lane/median widths divide by D within MapKit's ranges; a sidewalk
   below 20 cm is dropped. Placement yaw adds the angle (`quarter_turns` +
   `yaw_offset_mdeg`). Gable roofs stay gable only at right angles, otherwise flat.
   Road `design` is dropped; the transformed polyline is the road.
2. **Namespace.** Every ID becomes `part-<8 hex>-<local id>`; cut and join records use
   `part-<instance>-cut-…` / `-join-…`. A clash with an existing ID is refused.
3. **Replace.** Placements, POIs and buildings touching the footprint are removed.
   Ground roads, repetitions and barriers are cut at the footprint edge (bridges,
   elevated roads, tunnels and underpasses pass over or under whole); a cut road end gets a
   new node at the edge (height interpolated), stubs under 50 map cm are dropped and
   nodes left unused under the footprint are removed. Zones and surface areas keep only
   their outside part; when the footprint lies wholly inside an area, the area is split
   through the footprint so no piece needs a hole. Zone exclusions keep their part
   inside each piece and outside the footprint. Water bodies and gimmicks are not
   touched; a conflict with them is reported by MapKit.
4. **Join mouths.** Each mouth joins the nearest free ground-road end (a node used by
   one road and by no structure) within 20 real metres (÷D), ties going to the end
   nearest in height, else the nearest point of another ground road within that
   distance, which is split there. The mouth road gains a straight connector to the
   target at any angle; a mouth with no target keeps its free end. The status line
   reports joined/total mouths.
5. **Credits.** Part attributions missing from the map are added in the same command.

Placed records are selected afterwards, so the plan's move/duplicate/delete apply.

## Built-in set

Nine in-house parts (MIT, no OSM-derived data) in `parts/*.json`, generated by
`python scripts/city_parts_source.py parts`: four-way crossing, T junction and
roundabout (7 m two-lane roads with sidewalks and mouths at the footprint edge),
apartment, low-rise (gable houses) and shopping blocks, park, parking lot with an
access-road mouth, and plaza. Builtin furniture keeps its game size while the part
shrinks, so the generator spaces trees and benches to stay clear at 1:8 and any angle.
The export presets include `parts/*.json`.

## My parts

**Save selection as part…** turns the plan selection into a part in the user library
`Documents/MiniEarthure maps/parts/<id>.json` (`id` = `user-<10 hex>`; existing files
are never overwritten). Roads bring their end nodes; placements with document-owned
GLB assets are skipped; the origin is the selection's centre, heights are above its
lowest point and everything is multiplied back by D. Road ends used by one selected road
become mouths. The footprint is the convex hull grown by half the widest road (at
least 1 m) plus 1 m.

When any selected record came from an import, regional source or another part
(`import-`, `regional-`, `part-` IDs), the map's attributions are copied as
`{source, license, notice: ""}` (the `#layer` suffix removed): credits travel, import
metadata never does, so placing a part never changes the target map's 1:D provenance.
Purely hand-drawn selections carry none. Clicking a My parts entry arms it and makes
it the target of **Rename…** (rewrites the name through a verified copy and a kept
backup) and **Delete** (removes the file after confirmation). Writes are read back
before they are published. Unreadable files are reported by name in the status line.

## Validation and limits

`tests/city_parts_validator.gd` covers every built-in at 1:1, 1:3, 1:5 and 1:8 and
0°/15°/45°/60° (one Undo, namespace, undo restore), placement yaw and rotation sign,
gable flattening, replace cutting a street and joining both mouths, a tunnel kept
whole, clipped zone exclusions, free-end and road-split joins, 1:3 scaling, capture
with mouths and credits, save/no-overwrite/rename/place/delete, strict reader
rejections and file-named read errors, plus the Parts panel (arming, rotation, click
placement, Escape, rename/delete dialogs) and saved shortcuts surviving the new Q/E
defaults; `editor_mode_validator` covers the group layout.

Placement is plan-only (no 3D ghost). Parts sit flat at the height under their
origin; on sloped terrain the connectors absorb the difference. User checks: placing
and joining parts on real-city maps, rotation and alignment feel, and saving parts from
imported districts.
